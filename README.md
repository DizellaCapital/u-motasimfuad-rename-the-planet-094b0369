make.fun bounty entry: one original line renaming a planet of our solar
system and saying why, at most 200 characters. The line is the whole entry.

## The line

> Earth is now 'The Airbnb' — 8 billion guests, nobody does the dishes, and the host hasn't replied in 4.5 billion years.

119 characters. Earth was the one planet nobody had renamed yet in this
bounty — Venus and Saturn were already crowded with seven entries each —
and it is the only planet the joke fits: the only inhabited one, so its
8 billion people are guests, and its 4.5-billion-year age is the host who
never replies. The name and the why land together as one idea, not as a
pun on the planet's real name.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py  # writes entry.txt to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR (fallback /output)
```

`main.py` (stdlib only) validates the line is 1–200 characters on a single
line, names the planet and its new name, joins name to why, and ends with a
full stop, writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (fallback `/output`),
then reads it back to verify the written bytes match.
